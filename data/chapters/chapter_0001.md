# Chapter 1: Romance Dawn —The Dawn of the Adventure—

Arc: Romance Dawn Arc

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1 (CC-BY-SA)

## Cover page

Color Spread: Luffy, Nami, and the Red Hair Pirates party in a hoard of treasure, seagulls flying overhead and Shanks' Jolly Roger proudly flying in the background.

## Short summary

During his execution, the Pirate King Gold Roger revealed that he left his treasure for the world to find it, triggering the start of the Great Pirate Era.

12 years later, a young boy named Monkey D. Luffy tries to join the Red Hair Pirates, but their captain Shanks rebuffs him. Shanks later discovers that Luffy unknowingly ate a Devil Fruit that his crew had stolen. The fruit, the Gomu Gomu no Mi, has turned Luffy's body into rubber but at the cost of making him unable to ever swim. After an incident between Shanks and the mountain bandit Higuma, Luffy confronts Higuma the next time he sees him and is captured. The Red Hair Pirates overpower the bandits, but Higuma escapes to sea with Luffy and attempts to drown him. Shanks arrives in time to save Luffy from an attacking Sea King, but loses his left arm in the process. Later, the Red Hair Pirates prepare to depart the village for good, and Luffy declares that he will set out to sea on his own and one day surpass Shanks as a pirate. Shanks gives Luffy his straw hat, telling the boy to return it once he has accomplished this goal.

10 years later, Luffy sets out to sea and defeats the Sea King. He goes over his objectives of forming a crew in order to become the Pirate King.

## Long summary

Gold Roger held the title of "Pirate King" after obtaining all the wealth, fame, and power a pirate could have. Though he wound up meeting his end in a public execution, his final words inspired the world to set out to sea as he declared that he had left all of his treasure behind, though he then warned that anyone who desired it would have to search the entire world. Roger's announcement was met with cheers from the crowd as he was put to death, and this marked the beginning of the Great Pirate Era.

12 years later, a crew called the Red Hair Pirates has made their base at the idyllic Foosha Village for the last year. On their ship, a 7-year-old village boy named Monkey D. Luffy expresses his frustration over the crew not taking him seriously, and declares that he will show them how tough he is. The pirates are mildly interested to see what he does, only to react with horror as Luffy takes a knife and stabs himself below his left eye.

Later, the crew relocates to the Partys Bar and starts drinking. After reprimanding Luffy for his stunt, the captain Shanks turns jollier as he declares that the boy will never be a pirate because he cannot swim. Luffy claims that his punch is as powerful as a pistol, but Shanks is not convinced. The other Red Hair Pirates are more sympathetic to Luffy as they tell him about the fun and freedom they have at sea, but Shanks reaffirms that Luffy is too young to join them. Shanks' first mate Benn Beckman then gently reminds Luffy that there are also many dangers at sea. The barkeeper Makino serves Luffy and Shanks some food, and Shanks tells the boy that his crew will likely depart the village permanently after two or three more voyages; Luffy says he will learn how to swim by then.

While Luffy eats a spiral-patterned fruit, the mountain bandit Higuma enters the bar with his gang and demands ten barrels of alcohol. Makino admits that the bar's supply has been depleted because the Red Hair Pirates drank it all. Shanks apologizes and offers Higuma the last unopened bottle, but Higuma takes it and smashes it over the pirate's head in a show of force. Higuma boasts that he is a wanted criminal worth 8,000,000 as he tries to provoke Shanks into a fight, but Shanks responds by picking the bottle shards off the floor. To humiliate Shanks further, Higuma takes out his sword and smashes the tableware off the bar before departing. Shanks assures Makino that he is fine, and he and his crew have a hearty laugh over the affair. This angers Luffy, who asks why Shanks did not fight back and calls him a disgrace to pirates. Shanks responds that not every provocation requires retaliation, but Luffy storms out to go find a new role model. Shanks grabs his arm to hold him up, but Luffy continues to walk away as his arm stretches to a superhuman length. The Red Hair Pirates immediately check on a chest that contained the Gomu Gomu no Mi—which they had taken from an enemy ship—and find it empty. Luffy confirms that he ate it, and a panicked Shanks tells him that this is one of the Devil Fruits: by eating it, his body has gained the properties of rubber, but is now rendered unable to swim for the rest of his life.

The day the Red Hair Pirates set sail on their next voyage, Luffy cheerfully heads into the village. The fishmonger wonders why he is so cheerful even though he will never be able to swim, but Luffy is content with this side effect because he enjoys being able to contort his rubbery body. Mayor Woop Slap reprimands Luffy for getting involved with the pirates, saying he would not allow the boy to become a pirate too and make the village look bad, but Luffy ignores him. A long time later, Luffy goes to the Partys Bar, and Makino asks if he misses the Red Hair Pirates. Luffy denies this, as he still disagrees with Shanks' inaction against Higuma. Right then, Higuma returns to the bar with his gang and demands drinks. Soon afterward, Makino rushes to Woop Slap to tell him that Luffy has been apprehended by the bandits after attempting to attack them. Higuma holds Luffy out in the street and marvels over his rubbery composition, which is rendering him impervious to his punches and kicks. Higuma throws Luffy to the ground and holds him under his foot, and as the mayor arrives to try to resolve the situation, Luffy's continued defiance causes Higuma to try to kill him with his sword.

Right then, however, Shanks approaches the scene having just returned from his voyage. Higuma thinks little of the pirate's return, and one of his henchmen points his gun at Shanks. The captain remains calm and simply says that the bandit is risking his life, as Lucky Roux proceeds to suddenly shoot him in the head. The bandits protest that the crew's ambush was unfair, but Shanks and his men say that they follow no combat code, as they are pirates. Shanks then turns deadly serious as he declares that the bandits can humiliate him all they want, but he will not stand for anyone hurting his friend. Higuma is still unfazed, saying pirates do not compare to mountain bandits; he orders his men to attack, but Beckman says he will handle them alone. He overpowers the first assailant by sticking his cigarette in his eye, then swiftly dispatches the remaining bandits with the butt of his rifle before pointing it at Higuma; Luffy and the villagers are left stunned at Beckman's display of strength. Higuma immediately grows fearful, but is able to escape with Luffy in tow by throwing a smoke bomb into the ground. Shanks panics over losing Luffy, but Lucky Roux assures that they will find him.

Higuma escapes out to sea with Luffy in a small rowboat and decides to kill the boy; he easily dodges one of Luffy's punches before kicking him overboard. It is revealed that Luffy attacked Higuma in the Party's Bar because the bandit had been denigrating Shanks' honor, showing that he still respects Shanks. However, Higuma does not notice a giant Sea King called the Lord of the Coast emerging behind him until it is too late, and the creature consumes him and the boat in a single bite. The Lord of the Coast then notices Luffy and moves to eat him, but Shanks arrives in time to pull the boy away before scaring away the beast with just a look. Shanks had his left arm bitten off while pulling Luffy to safety, but he remains calm and consoles Luffy as the boy cries over his sacrifice; Shanks says that it was a small price to pay.

Realizing that Shanks is a great man, even though he is not yet strong enough, Luffy comes to understand why he cannot go out to sea with the Red Hair Pirates. As the crew prepares for their next voyage, Shanks reveals that they will not be returning to the village afterward. Luffy declares that he will set out to sea on his own one day, and Shanks jokingly repeats his line that there is no way Luffy can become a pirate. Luffy defiantly responds, declaring he will get a crew stronger than Shanks', have the most treasure in the world, and become the Pirate King. Impressed by Luffy's desire to surpass him, Shanks takes off his straw hat and places it on Luffy's head, asking him to keep it safe and return it once he has become a great pirate. As Shanks heads to his crew and ship, Beckman expresses high expectations for Luffy's future, and Shanks says that Luffy reminds him of himself as a youth. The Red Hair Pirates then raise anchor and set sail from Foosha Village for the final time.

10 years later, a 17-year-old Luffy sets out to sea himself from the very same harbor as Makino, Woop Slap, and the other villagers look on. As he rows away on a small dinghy, Luffy is once again confronted by the Lord of the Coast. However, he easily blasts the creature away with a powerful punch to the side of its head, aptly named Gomu Gomu no Pistol. As he looks out at the horizon, Luffy contemplates his objectives as a pirate. In particular, he wishes to gather a crew of about 10 people and get a pirate flag. Luffy then warns the world to look out as he declares that he will become the Pirate King.

## Notes & trivia

*As is standard for any new series premiere in Weekly Shonen Jump, it contains many more pages than the weekly average (and as of  remains the single longest chapter of the series).
**Its color content is also significantly higher than average, with the first four pages (the color spread and the first two "story" pages) being printed in full color, and the next twelve in a red-and-gray "part color" palette.
*This chapter is sometimes called "Romance Dawn, Version 3" because of the Romance Dawn one-shots, titled Romance Dawn, Version 1 and Romance Dawn, Version 2 that are different versions of the prologue for the One Piece manga.
*According to One Piece Magazine Vol.10, Nami's presence in the cover but not the chapter proper is a remnant of Oda's original plans for her to be Luffy's first crewmate, akin to the first and second Romance Dawn drafts.
**Despite this concept being scrapped for the manga, Nami would become Luffy's first crewmate in later non-canon content:
***Nami can become Luffy's first crewmate in the video game Birth of Luffy's Dream Pirate Crew!, which allows the player to go through story arcs in different noncanonical orders. 
***Nami would later be depicted as the first person Luffy recruits in the video game One Piece: Grand Adventure, this time unavoidably.
***In the live-action series, Nami and Roronoa Zoro join Luffy at the same time as his first crewmates, rather than Zoro joining on his own.
**In the anime's first episode, which adapts Chapter 2, Nami is the first member of the Straw Hat Pirates to appear, even before Luffy.
*Though Higuma enters Partys Bar by kicking a door clean off the hinge, the same door is mysteriously intact when he leaves only minutes later. When a reader questioned this, Oda half-jokingly invented a traveling carpenter and credited him with fixing the door.
**Similarly, the tooth that Luffy loses during Higuma's beating reappears throughout the rest of the chapter at random.
*The events of the sixth and final chapter of Shokugeki no Sanji occur somewhat parallel to this chapter.
