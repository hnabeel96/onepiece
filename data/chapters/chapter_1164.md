# Chapter 1164: Davy Blood

Arc: Elbaph Arc

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1164 (CC-BY-SA)

## Cover page

Cover Page Request: "Perona chasing a rabbit with a watch" by PN Tomari Shinonome.

## Short summary

Rocks resists his transformation and taunts Nerona Imu about Davy D. Jones and Joy Boy, which enrages Imu. Imu declares that he will subjugate Elbaph and Harald, much to Rocks' anger, and once again tells him to kill his family as Rocks' transformation completes. Rocks, in his demonic form, attacks Kaidou and Big Mom, and clashes with Whitebeard, destroying the mountain on God Valley. Rocks' nearly kills his own wife and son, but Kuma manages to teleport them and himself away from God Valley. Rocks kills several people till Garp and Roger confront him to stop him. They hear Rocks' voice, begging them to kill him.

## Long summary

Everyone watches as Rocks screams in pain due to the transformation process, but surprisingly, Rocks was doing his best to resist transforming, to a point Imu called him persistent. Rocks tells Imu not to underestimate the strength of a human being, and he proudly declares his belief that Davy D. Jones was once the king of the world. Imu is enraged by Rocks' words and declares that Jones was never the king of anything, but the animosity between the two of them has nothing to do with Rocks. Rocks, however, does not believe that because of how panicked Imu seems to be over something as simple as a family name. Rocks asks Imu if he has been terrified for years at the thought that someone would inherit Jones' will and fulfill the "promise" he spoke of. He then asks Imu what terrifies him more: Jones or Joy Boy.

Imu demands Rocks silence himself and announces that once he eliminates him, "their" kind will never be seen again. Imu then tells Rocks that he has found the perfect soldiers, which are the giants of Elbaph, as they are currently harmless and internally conflicted. Rocks becomes enraged and demands Imu leave Harald out of his plan because he only wants peace, but Imu says that Harald's desire for peace will allow him to mold Harald into the perfect puppet, just like he is molding him into one. Imu once again orders Rocks to end his own bloodline and everyone else on the island as he slams him on the ground, thus completing the transformation.

Linlin and Kaidou get too close to Rocks wondering what happened to him, but Rocks stands up and declares there is blood he needs to exterminate. In an instant, Rocks grabs Kaidou and slams him into the mountain, prompting Linlin to attack him. Unfortunately, Rocks also easily dispatches her as well. Newgate stands his ground and matches Rocks' attack with his own, causing a shockwave so powerful it shatters the northeast mountain and causes it to crumble. After that, Rocks continued to chase after his wife and son.

The Marines are ordered to keep their distance from the island and prioritize the safe evacuation of the World Nobles. Everyone else continues to try and flee with their lives and whatever treasure they were able to secure. Elsewhere, Dragon despairs over his inability to rescue baby Shanks, while Ivankov tells Kuma to stop as he has saved enough people, but Kuma insists there are more. John survives Ganzui's attack and manages to escape with some treasure. Rocks, meanwhile, manages to reach Eris and Teach. When Eris sees him, Eris figures that Rocks lost his confrontation with Imu and apologizes for it, saying he did it to save them even though he was not ready. Regardless, Eris holds Teach closer to her and says they will jump into the sea and swim away if that is what it takes to survive. Just as she reaches the shore, though, the Marines stationed open fire on her. Thankfully, both her and Teach are teleported away by Kuma, who saw them earlier, before teleporting himself away. Rocks' slash ends up destroying the ship, though he still gets hit by the cannons.

Seeing the fire in the distance, Imu orders Rocks to continue killing everyone else on the island. Garp and Roger confront Rocks as they do not want him to hurt anymore of their people, but they see tears streaming down his face and they somehow hear his voice in their heads. Rocks begs to Garp and Roger to kill him, as the three begin the fated battle.

## Chapter Notes

*While Rocks fights to resist the Domi Reversi, he proclaims that Davy D. Jones was the previous king of the world, utterly infuriating Imu.
**As Imu tries to deny it, Rocks taunts him further by claiming Imu is afraid of Joy Boy and Davy Jones' "promise".
**Imu further elaborated that the reason why he wants to eliminate the Davy clan, is to make sure that Davy Jones never resurfaces to his world again.
**Imu counters that he has found the perfect soldiers this time, the Giants of Elbaph and their "pliable king".
**An angry Rocks demands that Harald be left alone, but succumbs to the Domi Reversi.
**Worried, Linlin and Kaidou approach the demonized Rocks, who attacks and incapacitates them.
**Whitebeard tries to stop Rocks, but their clashing attacks causes the northeast mountain of God Valley to collapse on top of the nearby armadas.
*All the remaining Marines, World Nobles, and pirates evacuate the island en masse.
**Taro seems to have taken the treasure chest containing Shanks.
**Shiki attacked Ganzui, taking some of the treasure.
*Just as Rocks catches up to Teach and Eris and prepares to kill them, Kuma pushes them away at the last moment before fleeing himself.
*Imu then orders Rocks to kill any last remaining person on the island.
*Garp and Roger confront Rocks.
**Garp and Roger hear Rocks begging them to kill him.
**Garp is confirmed to be capable of hearing the Voice of All Things.
