# Chapter 1127: Adventure in the Land of Mystery

Volume: 111  |  Arc: Elbaph Arc

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1127 (CC-BY-SA)

## Cover page

Oni Child Yamato's Golden Harvest Surrogate Pilgrimage, Vol 15: "The kidnappings weigh heavily on Inuarashi, Daimyo of Kuri."

## Short summary

Strange occurrences are happening in the mysterious land, with a fire that threatens the tree Yggdrasil and the country as a whole. In Bigstein Castle, Nami is under attack by giant animals and runs into Usopp, saving him with Zeus. As they theorize on how they got there and consider hallucinations, they find the danger is real and are attacked by a monster that transforms into a giant lion, but are saved by Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji.

## Long summary

In the mysterious land, the people try to put out a fire before it spreads to Yggdrasil, the World Tree. In addition, several large bees have been falling from the tree, clearly having been injured, which confuses the people even more, and they think about reporting this to the "Sun God". They also say that their Ear God has fallen, which is a shock to them because they consider it a giant.

Elsewhere, in what is known as Bigstein Castle, Nami has taken down one of the giant bees with help from Zeus. She desperately starts crying for her crewmates to save her as she has no idea where she currently is or how she got here. Suddenly, she hears Usopp's voice and starts heading towards him, but she trips over the bumpy floor and comes face-to-face with a giant hedgehog, which while cute startles her. The hedgehog, in turn, gets startled too and begins to angrily chase her. Nami does find Usopp, but he too is being attacked by a large animal, in this case a giant cat. The hedgehog catches up to Nami as she extends Zeus to zap it, while also falling in the process. Thankfully, Nami lands on the cat as the hedgehog gets zapped, and it falls onto the cat, also zapping it.

Unfortunately, this only enrages the cat, and it starts chasing Nami and Usopp as the two try to get their bearings. Usopp tells Nami that like her, he just woke up in the castle wearing a new outfit, but they both remember they were drinking a special type of alcohol given to them by the Giants on the ship, which they said is powerful enough to cause hallucinations. Eventually, the two reach a dead end, but Usopp becomes convinced that the cat chasing them is a hallucination and everything else is a dream, which makes him confident he will be okay, only for the cat to actually hit him, proving that it is not an illusion.

The cat's attack pushes the two off the edge. After they land, they see the cat has now transformed into a lion. Nami ends up using Usopp as a shield to protect her, much to his displeasure. Thankfully, however, Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji finally appear and simultaneously take out the lion with their Gomu Gomu no Elephant Whip, Kokujo O Tatsumaki and Épaule Strike, respectively, and the lion turns back into a cat as it passes out. Luffy says he will not eat the cat because the rabbit they ate was filling enough while Sanji comforts Nami, who claims that Usopp is dead now, which is not true. Luffy says that the other animals in the castle are tasty, they should try hunting them, but Zoro points out that locating the ship and the rest of the crew takes priority. Sanji, meanwhile, states they should exploit the giant bees they encountered, but Nami is left incredulous by how nonchalant everybody is regarding their new situation, once again asking where they are right now.

## Notes & trivia

## Chapter Notes

*The missing Straw Hats have been transported to a mysterious land, where the world tree Yggdrasil lies.
*Nami and Usopp reunite inside a location known as Bigstein Castle.
**Luffy, Zoro and Sanji rescue them from a large cat monster capable of transforming into a lion.

## Characters
*The technique used to defeat Iscat, Ifrit Jambe Épaule Gear 4 Gomu Gomu no Elephant Three Sword Style Kokujo O Tatsumaki Whip Strike, intentionally mirrors a similar combination attack to defeat a Sandora Lizard in chapter 162. The chapter titles also mirror each other, respectively "Adventure in the Land of Mystery" and "Adventure in the Kingdom of Sand" (in Japanese, the same word is used for both "Land" and "Kingdom").
