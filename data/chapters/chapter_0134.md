# Chapter 134: Dr. Kureha

Arc: Drum Island Arc

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_134 (CC-BY-SA)

## Short summary

Dalton reveals that Wapol was Drum Island's tyrannical king who abandoned his people during Blackbeard's attack, but is now returning to reclaim his throne. Meanwhile, Luffy and Sanji encounter hostile Lapahns while carrying a sick Nami up the mountain. Elsewhere, the eccentric Dr. Kureha cures a dying boy and demands a steep payment.

## Long summary

Dalton is surprised to learn that the Straw Hats know about Wapol. Usopp responds that he tried to attack them a couple of days ago, and Vivi reveals that she met him a while ago at the Levely. Vivi's comment prompts shock from Dalton as to her true identity. Vivi attempts to cover up her mistake and changes the subject. Dalton then explains that Wapol was the king of the country but fled when pirates arrived, which surprises Vivi as to how a king can be so uncaring towards his people.

Meanwhile, Luffy carries a feverish Nami through the snow, alongside Sanji. As they flee from the attack of a baby Lapahn, Luffy and Sanji trade absurd theories: Luffy claims to have heard in his village's tavern that people in snowy countries do not sleep because, if they fall asleep, they die. While dodging attacks from hostile Lapahn in the snow, Sanji argues that rubbing skin for warmth makes women's skin soft and pale. After Sanji easily dispatches a baby Lapahn, a pack of adult Lapahns appears to block their path.

Meanwhile, Wapol has finally found Drum Kingdom. At the same time, Dr. Kureha arrives at a tavern in a neighboring village to treat a young boy suffering from a fatal illness. Using her unorthodox medical expertise, she successfully cures him, but her aggressive demeanor and demand for an exorbitant fee shock his father and the local villagers. Despite their outrage, the recovered boy expresses his gratitude, and Kureha takes her payment before leaving.

## Notes & trivia

* Coincidentally, back when Buggy was an apprentice on Gol D. Roger's ship, he mentioned to Shanks having heard that Blackbeard had never slept a single day in his life. It was likely Shanks or someone from his crew who recounted this to Luffy at Partys Bar.
