# Backtest: Kanjuro is the traitor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Confirmed (ch 974); used to validate the method |
| **Blind window** | Chapters 700–973 only |
| **Result** | Method picked Kanjuro before the reveal |

## Purpose

Check that the theory method works by hiding everything from the reveal
onward and seeing whether the earlier chapters point to the right answer.

## Step 1: prove a leak exists

| Ch | Signal |
|---|---|
| 936 | Enemy knows the secret tattoo signal; Shinobu suspects a leak |
| 955 | Orochi has a report on the alliance and knows the meeting point moved to Port Tokage |
| 954 | That move was made only within the inner circle |
| 958 | Alliance reaches Tokage: no ships, no allies |
| 970 | Kaidou had a spy in Oden's castle 20 years ago |
| 973 | Inuarashi and Nekomamushi accuse each other of being the spy |

The Tokage leak narrows suspects to whoever was present in ch 954.

## Step 2: narrow the suspects

| Suspect | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Straw Hats, Law | Out: outsiders, joined late |
| Momonosuke | Out: child, the heir the plan exists for |
| Kin'emon | Out: a traitor leader would simply sink the plan |
| Inuarashi, Nekomamushi | Out: mutual accusation is a misdirect; Neko absent in ch 954 |
| Raizo, Kiku, Kawamatsu, Ashura | Out: no unexplained solo time near the enemy |
| **Kanjuro** | **Kept** |

## Step 3: Kanjuro's pattern

- **Opportunity:** alone in the Flower Capital (ch 929); leaves alone with
  Yasuie's body around the time of the leak (ch 951).
- **Means:** drawings that come to life (ch 754 onward), ideal for decoys.
- **Gaps:** held captive on Dressrosa yet walks out of a wall at will (ch 754).
- **"Accidents":** his ladders help a hostile crowd climb (ch 756).
- **Distancing:** claims not to know the capital's biggest yakuza boss (ch 934).
- **Backstory:** joined Oden late, as a thief known as the Goblin of Kibi (ch 962).

## Method lessons

1. **Start from the leak, not the suspect.** Pin down what information
   escaped, then who could have known it.
2. **Score suspects on opportunity, means, motive and narrative signals;**
   drop anyone with zero opportunity.
3. **A loud red herring is evidence.** Oda staged the Inu/Neko accusation one
   chapter before the reveal.
4. **Beware hindsight in the source.** Wiki summaries get edited after
   reveals (ch 929 calls Kanjuro's cover a "masquerade"). Weight what
   happened over how the summary words it.
